# 📊 Power BI Dashboards — Collection

> Power BI · DAX · Excel · Data Visualization

A collection of 3 production-grade Power BI dashboards covering
retail sales, e-commerce KPIs, and corporate sales performance.
Each dashboard was built from raw Excel data — cleaned, modeled,
and visualized to deliver actionable business insights through
interactive reports.

---

## 📁 Dashboards Included

| Dashboard | Domain | Key Metrics |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Ecommerce Sales Report | E-Commerce | Revenue, Profit, AOV, Payment Mode |
| Super Store Sales | Retail | Sales, Profit, Delivery Time, Segments |
| SN Corporation | Corporate Retail | Total Sales, Cost, Profit, Regional |

---

## 1️⃣ Ecommerce Sales Report Dashboard

An interactive e-commerce KPI dashboard tracking sales performance,
profit trends, customer behavior, and payment patterns across
product categories and cities.

### Key Metrics
- **Total Revenue** — ₹438K across all transactions
- **Total Profit** — ₹37K with monthly profit trend tracking
- **Total Quantity Sold** — 5,615 units
- **Average Order Value (AOV)** — ₹121K

### Visuals Included
- Sum of Profit by Sub-Category — Printers and Bookcases lead
- Profit by Month — highlights seasonality and loss months
- Sum of Amount by City — Indore leads followed by Mumbai and Pune
- Sum of Amount by Customer Name — top revenue customers ranked
- Payment Mode Distribution — COD 44%, UPI 21%, Debit 13%,
  Credit 12%
- Quantity by Category — Clothing 63%, Electronics 21%,
  Furniture 17%
---

## 2️⃣ Super Store Sales Dashboard

A multi-year sales performance dashboard with regional filtering
covering 2019–2021. Built to track sales trends, profitability,
delivery efficiency, and customer segment breakdown.

### Key Metrics
- **Total Sales** — $1.6M across all regions
- **Total Profit** — $175K
- **Average Delivery Time** — 3.93 days

### Visuals Included
- Sales by Category — Office Supplies $643K, Technology $470K,
  Furniture $451K
- Sales by Sub-Category — Phones, Chairs, and Binders lead
- Sum of Profit by Month and Year — 2019–2021 trend comparison
- Sum of Sales by Month and Year — seasonal patterns across years
- Sales by Segment — Consumer 48%, Corporate 33%, Home Office 19%
- Sales by Payment Mode — COD 43%, Online 35%, Cards 22%
- Regional filter — Central, South, East, West

---

## 3️⃣ SN Corporation Sales Dashboard

A corporate retail sales dashboard for SN Corporation covering
2017–2018 with geographic distribution, customer-level breakdown,
and shipping mode analysis.

### Key Metrics
- **Total Sales** — $504.6K
- **Total Cost** — $247.9K
- **Total Profit** — $256.6K

### Visuals Included
- KPI Cards — Total Sales, Cost, and Profit at a glance
- Customer Table — Sales, Cost, and Profit per customer ranked
- Sum of Sales by Sub-Category — Bookcases and Chairs lead
- Sum of Quantity by Region — South 56%, Central 22%, North 22%
- Count of Orders by Year, Quarter, and Ship Mode — Economy
  shipping dominates across both years
- Geographic Map — Sales distribution across European locations
- Slicers — Order Date range and Ship Mode filter
